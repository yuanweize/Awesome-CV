# Role strategy and readiness

Keep career direction, current evidence, and immediate job readiness separate.

## Three independent questions

1. **Interest:** Does the person want to pursue this responsibility pattern?
2. **Evidence:** Which verified claims transfer to it today?
3. **Readiness:** Is the target title a direct, credible, or stretch application now?

Never delete or suppress an interested role family merely because current evidence is
thin. Record the gap and the smallest next proof that would improve readiness. Equally,
never turn interest, installed software, course exposure, or AI-tool use into an
eligible capability claim without a defensible action and scope.

## Role-family design

Create a separate role family when the job has materially different responsibilities,
proof ordering, CV identity, or interview preparation. Keep adjacent titles in one
family, and list titles that need stronger evidence under `stretch_titles`.

Use `career_preferences.role_interests` as planning memory. It affects which jobs the
agent recommends and which gaps it helps close; it is never résumé prose and is not
evidence.

## AI-assisted work versus AI/ML engineering

Treat these as different facts:

- AI-assisted engineering or agent orchestration can be evidenced by a repeatable
  workflow, authored artifacts, review gates, tests, and the person's ability to
  explain decisions and failures.
- Applied AI integration can be evidenced by implemented inference backends, model or
  provider selection, structured outputs, evaluation, fallback, latency/cost/privacy
  trade-offs, or deployment.
- ML engineering or research additionally needs defensible data, training/evaluation,
  experiment, statistical, or model-lifecycle work.

Using an agent is useful evidence for the first category, but does not by itself prove
the second or third.

## Hardware and electronics evidence

Distinguish hardware validation and IoT integration from FPGA/PCB/circuit design.
A thesis integrating ESP32 devices, sensors, secure messaging, and measured load is
strong evidence for IoT integration and hardware validation. Coursework repositories
can support bounded VHDL/FSM or microcontroller claims when authorship and lab scope are
clear. Do not generalize those facts into professional PCB design, RF design, production
firmware ownership, or electrical authorisation.

## Personal domain work and professional transfer

Personal work may be CV-eligible when it records a repeatable action the person can
defend, uses explicit personal-scope language, and has a self-reported evidence record.
For example, owner maintenance with a manufacturer diagnostic tool can support bounded
diagnostics, fault-finding, and repair-verification claims. It does not become OEM
employment, dealership authority, vehicle-development ownership, or proof of protocols
and safety processes that were not actually implemented.

Create a dedicated role family when a desired domain changes the proof order. A vehicle
systems application may lead with personal diagnostics, professional device/GNSS test,
and electronics integration, while keeping industrial commissioning as an adjacent
differentiator. Do not force that identity into generic field service merely because
some evidence is transferable.

## Recommendation rule

For a high-interest family, do not recommend `defer` solely because it is marked
`stretch`. Compare the actual JD must-haves with verified claims. Recommend:

- `apply` when the central responsibilities have credible evidence;
- `stretch` when adjacent evidence transfers and the missing requirements are
  learnable or non-critical;
- `defer` only when a hard requirement or core responsibility is unsupported.

Always state the smallest next project, benchmark, course artifact, or interview drill
that would move a desired title from stretch to credible.
