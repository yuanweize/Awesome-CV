# Golden Portfolio delivery

Golden Portfolios are complete, reviewed recruiter products belonging to their registered
Packs. They are not regenerated from a JD and are not factual authority.

## Application policy

- Select the Golden Portfolio belonging to the chosen Pack.
- For a portal that benefits from a shorter attachment, select, omit, or reorder approved
  pages. Do not rewrite project facts, captions, screenshots, or diagrams for the vacancy.
- Prefer standalone CV for an ATS resume field and a separate Portfolio when an additional
  attachment is allowed. Combined is usually better for direct recruiter or hiring-manager
  delivery than for ATS ingestion.
- Record the selected page/module IDs and order in the private application manifest or
  notes. The Golden master Portfolio stays complete and unchanged.

## Evidence and presentation

- Every case must remain mapped to eligible claim IDs and the governed asset index.
- A screenshot or repository page illustrates an already supported claim; it never proves
  a new feature, outcome, scale, scope, or personal proficiency.
- Preserve contractor, field, academic, personal/open-source, owner-operated, and
  AI-assisted boundaries where material.
- Repository languages and ratios are source-composition data, not personal skills.
  Mutable counts must be dated.
- Important text and links must remain selectable; images cannot carry the only copy of
  a claim or URL.

## Asset and privacy rules

- `assets/portfolio/raw-private/` (and legacy `raw/`) contains original local evidence
  and is private by default.
- `assets/portfolio/curated/` contains recruiter-facing crops/derivatives; it still needs
  explicit privacy review before publication.
- `assets/portfolio/generated/` contains user-specific designed diagrams that preserve
  governed facts; it remains local unless separately approved for publication.
- Tracked public-demo visuals belong under `examples/assets/` and must be synthetic or
  explicitly public-safe.
- Never sanitise only through PDF clipping: the embedded source may remain extractable.
- Inspect both source pixels and every rendered page for credentials, tokens, private
  addresses, admin paths, customer/internal data, browser identity, and accidental old
  image variants.

After a delivery cut, re-run link, text, page, rendering, bundle, and privacy checks. The
cut is an application artifact; it does not become a new Golden Portfolio version.
